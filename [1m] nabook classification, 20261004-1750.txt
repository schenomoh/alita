# Nabook Classification System (Levels 1 & 2)

The Nabook classification system adapts the domain distribution of classic system design for human memory (Miller's Law) and digital extensibility through hybrid notation and sparse letter indexing.

## Structural Design Principles

1. **Ergonomic Memory Limits (Miller's Law):**
   * Level 1 maintains 9 top-level categories.
   * Level 2 contains 6 main sub-nodes per domain, respecting human working memory limits ($7 \pm 2$).

2. **Unambiguous Character Set:**
   * Letters `i`, `l`, and `o` are explicitly excluded from Level 2 indexing to avoid confusion with digits `1` and `0`.

3. **Sparse Indexing & Future Expansion:**
   * Non-consecutive letter spacing (`a`, `c`, `f`, `h`, `j`, `m`) leaves open gaps (`b`, `d`, `e`, `g`, `k`, etc.) allowing seamless future additions without re-indexing existing nodes.
